This program was developed for GB Manufacturing, a fictional company experiencing issues with tools and equipment going missing from its warehouse. The purpose of the system is to provide an accurate and reliable method for tracking company equipment whenever it leaves the warehouse.

Before removing any equipment, employees are required to log into the system and identify the item or items they are taking. The system records the employee associated with the equipment, along with the date and time the equipment was checked out. When the equipment is returned, the system records the return date and time as well.

All equipment and transaction information is stored in a MySQL database, allowing the company to maintain an organized record of equipment usage and movement. This information can be reviewed to determine who was responsible for equipment at a particular time and to help identify discrepancies or unauthorized activity.

The overall goal of the system is to improve accountability, reduce the likelihood of equipment being lost or stolen, and provide management with accurate records that can be reviewed and reported through the appropriate company channels when necessary.
